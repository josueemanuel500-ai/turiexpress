Terms and Conditions

Last updated: November 26, 2026

These terms apply to the Nissan Urvan rental service offered by Turi Express MX and to the use of this website. By sending a rental request, you accept them. This is a courtesy translation; if there is any difference, the Spanish version prevails.

1. About the service

Turi Express MX, based in Mérida, Yucatán, Mexico, rents Nissan Urvan vans for transfers, groups and multi-day trips.

2. A request is not a confirmed booking

When you send the form on this site we temporarily hold your dates for a few hours, but your rental is not confirmed yet.

A booking is confirmed only when Turi Express MX staff expressly confirm it on WhatsApp, after checking availability, price and conditions. If the hold expires without confirmation, the dates are released.

We may decline a request when the requirements in section 3 are not met, the van is not available, or the information provided is incorrect or incomplete.

3. Rental requirements

  • Minimum age: 25 years.
  • A valid driver’s license for this type of vehicle.
  • Valid official ID: passport or Mexican INE.
  • Security deposit, refunded when the van is returned as agreed.

Documents are checked at pick-up. If they are not presented, the rental may be cancelled.

4. Price, currency, deposit and payment

Prices shown on the site are estimates. The final rate, deposit and payment method are agreed on WhatsApp before the booking is confirmed.

All charges are made in Mexican pesos (MXN). The US dollar amount shown on the site is an approximate reference based on a daily exchange rate and is not a price in that currency.

The deposit is refunded at the end of the rental after the van is inspected, provided it is returned in the same condition and with no outstanding charges (missing fuel, damage, fines, late hours, etc.).

5. Use of the van

  • Fuel: the van must be returned with the same fuel level.
  • Mileage: unlimited within the metropolitan area. Trips beyond it must be agreed in advance.
  • Pick-up and return: date, time and place are confirmed on WhatsApp. The van is inspected with photos at pick-up and return.
  • Driver: only the person named in the rental contract may drive, unless authorized in writing.
  • The van may not be sublet, used for illegal purposes, or used to tow or carry loads beyond its capacity.

6. Renter’s responsibility

  • Damage caused by misuse, negligence or breach of these terms.
  • Fines, traffic tickets and charges incurred during the rental.
  • Returning the van on the agreed date and time; late returns may incur extra charges.
  • Reporting any accident, breakdown or incident immediately.

Insurance coverage details are explained when the rental contract is signed.

7. Cancellations and date changes

If you need to cancel or change your dates, let us know on WhatsApp as soon as possible. Cancellation conditions and deposit handling are explained when your booking is confirmed.

Turi Express MX may cancel a confirmed booking due to force majeure or mechanical failure; in that case we will offer another available van or refund what you paid.

8. Use of this website

You agree to provide truthful information and not to send fake or automated requests that block availability without a real intention to rent.

Site content (text, images, brand and design) belongs to Turi Express MX and may not be reproduced without permission.

9. Personal data

Your data is handled according to our Privacy Notice. We do not share it with third parties for commercial purposes and we delete it 12 months after the rental ends.

10. Language

This site is available in Spanish and English. The English version is a courtesy translation; the Spanish version prevails.

11. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms at any time. The current version is the one published on this page. Your rental is governed by the terms in force when your booking is confirmed.

12. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of Mexico. Any dispute will be submitted to the competent courts of Mérida, Yucatán.
