FAQ

Your questions, answered

What is the minimum age to rent a van?

25 years old, with a valid driver’s license and official ID (passport or Mexican INE).

Which documents do I need?

A valid driver’s license, official ID (passport or INE) and a security deposit that is refunded when you return the van.

Can I rent the van for just one day?

Yes. We rent by the day or by the week, depending on your trip or event.

How is my booking confirmed?

When you send your request we hold a van for a few hours. The booking is confirmed when our team replies on WhatsApp.

Can I pay in US dollars?

Prices are charged in Mexican pesos. We show an approximate US dollar amount for reference only; payment methods are arranged on WhatsApp.

Is mileage unlimited?

Yes, within the metropolitan area. If your trip goes beyond it, we agree on it with you before confirming.

Do you do transfers to Mérida airport?

Yes, we arrange group transfers to the airport with room for passengers and luggage.
