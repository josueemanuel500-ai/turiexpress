Data protection

Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This notice explains, in plain words, what data we collect, why, who we do not share it with, and when we delete it. This is a courtesy translation; if there is any difference, the Spanish version prevails.

1. Data controller

Turi Express MX, based in Mérida, Yucatán, Mexico, is responsible for your personal data under Mexico’s Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties. Contact: WhatsApp +52 999 509 5005.

2. What we collect

On this website, when you send a request: your name, phone (WhatsApp) and requested dates.

When the rental is formalized, in person: details from your official ID and driver’s license, address, your signature on the contract, and photos of the van’s condition at pick-up and return.

We do not collect sensitive data or bank/card details through this site.

3. Why we use it

  • To register your request and hold a van for your dates.
  • To contact you on WhatsApp to confirm availability, price and conditions.
  • To prepare the rental contract and document the van’s condition.
  • For internal records of rentals and payments, and to handle questions.

We do not use your data for advertising or marketing.

4. We do not share your data

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties for commercial purposes. Only these are involved:

  • Our own server: the booking system and its database run on a server managed directly by Turi Express MX.
  • Technical providers (processors): Hostinger hosts this website and Cloudflare secures the encrypted connection to the booking system. They process data only to provide that service.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): the conversation to confirm your rental is also subject to that platform’s policies.
  • Competent authorities: only when formally required by law.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data for 12 months after the rental ends. After that, the system automatically deletes your contact details, documents and photos. You can ask us to delete them sooner (section 6).

6. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion or objection to the processing of your data, and withdraw your consent. Message us on WhatsApp with your name, the phone used for the request and what you would like us to do. We reply within 20 business days, free of charge. If you are not satisfied, you may contact the competent Mexican data protection authority.

7. How we protect it

  • All communication is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • The public calendar only shows booked dates, never names or phone numbers.
  • Only authorized staff, each with their own login, can see rental details.
  • We make daily backups on our own server.

8. Cookies

This site does not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. Fonts and images are served from our own site. The exchange rate used to show US dollars is fetched by our server, not by your browser.

9. Changes

If we change this notice, we will publish the updated version on this page with a new date.

Questions about this notice? Message us on WhatsApp and we will gladly explain.
